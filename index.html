Everything you copy.

Trove is an encrypted clipboard history for macOS. Press ⌥ Space, type, and ↵ pastes it into the app you were in.

Download for Mac View on GitHub
Version 0.1.0 · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon · Free and open source (MIT)
or with Homebrew: brew install --cask gorkemblm/tap/trove

Fast to reach. Private by design.

A small panel over any app, made for the keyboard, with your history encrypted on your Mac.

⚡

Instant search

Text, links, images, files, colors and code. Search stays instant with tens of thousands of items.

🖼

Text in screenshots (OCR)

Images are read on your Mac, so a word inside a screenshot finds it, boxed in yellow.

⌘

Keyboard first

Jump to a group with /, to one app with @, to every action with ⌘K.

✎

Edit and transform

Change case, clean up spaces, sort lines, encode URLs and Base64, format JSON, then paste.

★

Snippets

Keep what you paste often under a name. Snippets never expire and are found by name.

⇪

Several at once

Pick items, paste them together, or drag them into any app or folder.

Your history stays yours

Trove has no network access. Only its separate update service can connect, to check for new versions, and it never sees your history.

Encrypted on disk

The database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256), images with AES-GCM. The key stays in your Keychain.

Secrets kept apart

API keys, tokens, card numbers and IBANs go to Hidden, sealed by the Secure Enclave and opened with Touch ID.

Your folder, your choice

Keep the history anywhere, even in a synced folder. It only ever holds encrypted files.

Moving from another app? Trove imports your history.
