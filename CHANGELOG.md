# Changelog

Every release of Trove. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## Unreleased

- **IP addresses** have their own group under Text: IPv4 and IPv6, with a prefix length or a port, alone or as a list. The details show the version and whether the address is public, private or local. Addresses already in the history move to the group.
- **Settings** and the other Trove windows open in front of the active app, also the first time.
- **⌘K** lists only actions: *How Trove Works* is in Settings › General and in the menu bar menu.

## 0.1.0

The first public release.

- **Clipboard history** for text, rich text, links, images, files, colors and code, opened with ⌥ Space over any app.
- **Search** across everything, including the text inside screenshots and images, read on your Mac.
- **Groups and apps:** jump to a group with `/`, to the items of one app with `@`.
- **Actions with ⌘K**, an **editor with text transforms** (⌘E), **named snippets** (⌘S).
- **Several items at once:** pick, paste together, delete, or drag them to any app.
- **Encryption:** SQLCipher database, AES-GCM image files, an optional recovery password for other Macs.
- **Hidden items:** API keys, tokens, card numbers and IBANs are recognized and sealed with the Secure Enclave; they open with Touch ID.
- **Import** from Raycast, Maccy and Alfred; **export** as an encrypted file, JSON or CSV.
- **English and Turkish**, with an animated tour of how Trove works.
